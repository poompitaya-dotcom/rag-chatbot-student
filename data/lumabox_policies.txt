LumaBox Customer Policies

Returns
Case 1, good condition online return: use EMS to send the item back; the customer pays the delivery cost.
Case 2, defective online return: a messenger will pick up the item within 10 km of the store; otherwise EMS is used and LumaBox pays the delivery cost.
Case 3, in-store return: visit the store between 8:00 and 17:00.

Delivery
If a parcel is lost, contact the courier's support first, since delivery is outsourced. If you can't reach them, contact LumaBox technical support instead.

Exchanges
LumaBox does not offer exchanges. To change to a different model, return the original item under the returns policy and place a new order; the refund and the new order are handled separately.

Order Tracking
To track an order, open My Orders on the LumaBox website and use the tracking number shown there. LumaBox technical support cannot locate a parcel without that tracking number.

Refund
LumaBox refunds are processed within 8 business days after the returned product passes inspection.

Technical Support
Available 24/7, every day.

Account
To reset a LumaBox password, select Forgot password on the sign-in page.
